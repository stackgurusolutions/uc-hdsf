About UC-HDSF 2026

The Department of Fashion Design, School of Architecture & Design, FOSTA, Manipal University Jaipur, India is organizing the UC-HDSF 2026 during October 22–23, 2026. UC-HDSF 2026 invites industry professionals, academicians, researchers, and students to submit original, high-quality, and previously unpublished research papers.

2

Days of Conference

50+

Expected Papers

200+

Expected Participants

Call for Papers

Submit your original research and join the global conversation on Design, Innovation, and Sustainability

Submission Guidelines

Researchers, academicians, and professionals are invited to submit their research abstracts and full papers for oral and poster presentations. Selected presentations will be appreciated with Cash Awards under "Best Paper Category" and "Outstanding Research Category".

Submission Requirements

  • • Original, unpublished research
  • • High-quality academic content
  • • Follow conference format guidelines
  • • Peer-review process

Presentation Formats

  • • Oral presentations
  • • Poster presentations
  • • Panel discussions
  • • Interactive sessions
