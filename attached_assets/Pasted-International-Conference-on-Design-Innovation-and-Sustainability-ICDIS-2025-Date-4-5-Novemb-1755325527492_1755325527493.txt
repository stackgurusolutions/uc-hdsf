International Conference on Design, Innovation, and Sustainability (ICDIS-2025)
📅 Date: 4-5 November 2025
📍 Venue: Department of Fashion Design, School of Architecture and Design, FoSTA, Manipal University Jaipur, India
________________________________________
About the Conference
The Department of Fashion Design, School of Architecture & Design, FOSTA, Manipal University Jaipur, India is going to organize the International Conference on Design, Innovation, and Sustainability (ICDIS-2025) during November 4-5, 2025. ICDIS-2025 invites industry professionals, academicians, researchers, and students to submit original, high-quality, and previously unpublished research papers. The conference aims to address key research challenges in the areas of Design, Innovation, and Sustainability, with a strong emphasis on aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The conference will provide a platform for showcasing significant research and fostering discussions across various disciplines related to design, innovation and sustainability practices. Eminent keynote speakers from across the globe will share their expertise, insights, and future directions. To ensure high-quality publications, the acceptance rate will be restricted to 20-25%. All submissions will be managed electronically using a recognized conference management platform.

Call for Papers
Researchers, academicians, and professionals are invited to submit their research abstracts and full papers for oral and poster presentations. Selected presentations (oral/poster) on each session/theme will be appreciated with the Cash Award under “Best Paper Category” and “Outstanding Research Category”.

📌 Full Paper Submission Deadline: 15th October 2025]

Authors must follow the submission guidelines provided on the official conference website. Submitted articles will undergo a peer-review process before acceptance.
